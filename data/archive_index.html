"In Flower This Week" Archive

These web pages are based on the In Flower This Week hand-out sheet that was prepared by a Gardens volunteer and made available at the Visitor Centre. It was produced weekly from 9 September 1985 until December 2013. It was then published every two weeks until 30 August 2016.

Back editions of the In Flower This Week (IFTW) hand-out sheets are available in this archive. The list starts with the final sheet published and goes back to 1997, with dates in (year-month-day) format.

Numbers before each plant name referred to temporary IFTW labels that were placed throughout the gardens.
Numbers in square brackets [ ] refer to garden bed Sections. The names of featured plants are in bold type.

Searching on a particular plant name results in a list of archive links, but these have the date built into the file name so it also gives a range of flowering times for any plants that caught the eye of the IFTW volunteers.
